HAZRA BAGAN LANE SARBOJANIN SRI SRI KALI PUJA SAMITY (Reg. No. S0060686) - FEATURE LIST
=====================================================================================

LOOK & FEEL
- Header with the full samity name, registration number and the round Maa Kali logo
- Maa Kali picture as logo, browser-tab icon and phone "Add to Home Screen" icon
- Fireworks animation in the background (can be switched off in the Data panel; automatically off if the device asks for reduced motion; pauses when the tab is hidden)
- Mobile-first design, light and dark mode, works on desktop too

BILLS TAB
- Search by name, address or bill number, with auto-complete suggestions and highlighted matches
- A search of only digits is an exact 2026 bill-number search
- Top boxes: 2026 Collected, 2026 Due, Bhog to be delivered, Bhog issued (with total boxes) and In-hand balance (tap to open Expenses)
- Filter buttons: All, 2026 Paid, 2026 Due, Bhog to be delivered, Bhog issued, Bill not issued, Premium members, 2025 Paid, 2025 Due
- Lists from the boxes/filters are sorted by 2026 bill number; "Show more" for long lists
- 272 bills from the 2025 Excel are built in and read-only (2025 amount, status, notes)

2026 EDITING (each bill)
- Mark Paid / Due
- Add or edit 2026 bill number and amount
- Bhog switches: "To be delivered" and "Issued" (optional, one, both or none; undo by switching off)
- "Issued" opens on 9 Nov 2026, 12:00 noon India time (checked by app and server)
- "Boxes issued" count when Bhog is issued
- Tags: Bill not issued, NEW · 2026 (manual entries), 2025 bill number
- Premium members: 2026 bill numbers 251-300 get a gold crown badge and card
- BHOG WITHOUT A BILL: Add Entry > "Bhog only (no bill)" records just a name and the number of bhog boxes issued (for people who have no bill). They are listed under the "Bhog without bill" button and under "Bhog issued", searchable by name, boxes can be edited, entries deleted. Same 9 Nov noon rule.
- TOTAL BHOG ISSUED: the "Bhog issued" box shows total boxes (with bill + without bill); the Bhog issued / Bhog without bill lists start with a summary card; Data > "Export bhog issued" downloads the full list with totals
- Add Entry form (manual entries for new members) with address auto-complete from existing addresses; manual entries can be deleted

EXPENSES TAB
- In-hand balance card = 2026 bill collection + other income - total expenses (turns red if negative), with the three figures listed
- Add Expense: paid to / for, optional note, Full or Partial payment, amount (no date to enter)
- Partial payment: optional Sub category text on every payment, "Add another payment" under the same name, every payment listed, Total payment line, delete a single payment
- Delete a whole expense
- Other income entries (e.g. Withdrawn from bank): source, note, amount; listed under "Other income", can be deleted
- Search with auto-complete across expenses and other income (name, note, amount); shows the total of matches

DATA & SHARING
- 2026 data saved online and visible to everyone (Netlify function + storage); falls back to this device only if sync is not set up
- Optional ADMIN_PIN: anyone can search, only people with the PIN can change data; PIN remembered per device; lock button in the bottom bar
- Saves are sent one at a time so quick edits never overwrite each other
- Export 2026 list (CSV: SL No, Name, Address, Amount, Payment status, bhog columns)
- Export expenses (CSV: every payment, other income, totals and in-hand balance)
- SORT: results have a Sort switch - "Bill no. ↑" or "Bhog issued ↓" (most bhog boxes first, people without a bill included). The Bhog issued and Bhog without bill lists open sorted by most bhog boxes first.
- FULL BACKUP / FULL RESTORE (Data panel): one file with ALL data - the 2025 bills and everything for 2026 - to rebuild the whole site on another site (see README)
- Backup (JSON, includes bills, bhog, expenses, other income) and Restore (replaces 2026 data; older backups keep current expenses/income)
- 2025 data is never changed
